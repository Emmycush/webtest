About CyberSafe Academy

CyberSafe Academy is an educational website designed to help beginners understand cybersecurity and stay safer online.

We provide simple lessons about online safety, phishing, passwords, account protection and other important cybersecurity topics.

Our goal is to make cybersecurity knowledge easy to understand and useful for students, beginners and everyday internet users.

Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, phones, networks, applications and personal information from digital threats.

Learning basic cybersecurity can help you avoid scams, protect your accounts and stay safer online.

    Cybersecurity Safety Tips

    • Use strong and unique passwords.
    • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
    • Keep your phone and applications updated.
    • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
    • Never share passwords or verification codes.

    • Back up important information.
