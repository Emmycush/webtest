    Password Security

    Strong passwords are an important part of protecting your online accounts.

    How to create a strong password

    • Use a long password with several characters.
    • Mix uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers and symbols.
    • Use different passwords for important accounts.
    • Avoid using easily guessed information such as your name or birthday.

    Consider using a trusted password manager to securely store your passwords.

    Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

    Two-factor authentication adds an extra layer of security to your online accounts.

    How 2FA works

    After entering your password, you may be asked for another verification method, such as a code from an authenticator app.

    • Enable 2FA on important accounts whenever possible.
    • Use an authenticator app when available.
    • Never share your verification codes with other people.

    Safe Internet Browsing

    Safe browsing habits can help protect you from scams, malware and other online threats.

    Tips for safer browsing

    • Check the website address before entering personal information.
    • Avoid clicking suspicious links or unexpected downloads.
    • Keep your browser and device software updated.
    • Use secure websites when entering sensitive information.
    • Be careful when using public Wi-Fi networks.

    Cybersecurity Safety Tips

    • Use strong and unique passwords.
    • Enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
    • Keep your phone and applications updated.
    • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
    • Never share passwords or verification codes.
    • Use secure Wi-Fi networks when possible.
    • Back up important information.

    Why Cybersecurity Matters

    Cybersecurity helps protect individuals, businesses, schools and organizations from data theft, financial loss and other digital threats.
