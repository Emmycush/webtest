ACCOUNT SECURITY

Two-Factor Authentication

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of security to an account. After entering a password, users may be asked for another verification method, such as an authenticator code.

🔑 PRACTICAL HABITS

Protect your accounts

Enable 2FA on important accounts whenever possible, use an authenticator app when available, and never share verification codes with other people.

🛡️ SECURITY PRINCIPLE

More than a password

A second verification factor can provide additional protection when a password has been exposed or guessed.

ONLINE SAFETY

Safe Internet Browsing

Safer browsing habits can help reduce exposure to scams, malicious downloads, and other online threats.

🌐 BROWSING HABITS

Check before you click

Check website addresses before entering sensitive information, avoid suspicious links and unexpected downloads, and keep browsers and devices updated.

📶 NETWORK SAFETY

Be careful on public Wi-Fi

Public networks can introduce additional risks. Be cautious when accessing sensitive accounts and avoid trusting unknown networks automatically.

ABOUT CYBERSAFE ACADEMY

Cybersecurity education should be practical.

CyberSafe Academy is designed to make cybersecurity easier to understand for beginners, students, and everyday internet users.

Our goal is simple: explain important security concepts clearly and give learners opportunities to practice safe defensive skills.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is CyberSafe Academy for beginners?

Yes. The learning experience is designed to start with fundamental concepts and gradually introduce more practical cybersecurity topics.

Are the interactive demonstrations safe?

The demonstrations are designed as educational simulations. They do not require you to attack real systems or provide real account credentials.

Do I need cybersecurity experience?

No. You can begin with the fundamentals and progress at your own pace.
