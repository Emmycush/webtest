Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Two-factor authentication adds another layer of security to an account. After entering a password, users may be asked for another verification method, such as an authenticator code.

  • Enable 2FA on important accounts whenever possible.
  • Use an authenticator app when available.
  • Never share verification codes with other people.

Safe Internet Browsing

Safer browsing habits can help reduce exposure to scams, malicious downloads and other online threats.

  • Check website addresses before entering sensitive information.
  • Avoid suspicious links and unexpected downloads.
  • Keep browsers and devices updated.
  • Be careful when using public Wi-Fi.

Cybersecurity Safety Tips

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Keep phones, computers and applications updated.
  • Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Use secure Wi-Fi networks when possible.
  • Back up important information.

Why Cybersecurity Matters

Cybersecurity helps protect individuals, businesses, schools and organizations from data theft, fraud, unauthorized access and other digital threats.
