Why these clues matter

  • Urgency: Attackers often use pressure to make people act before thinking.
  • Generic greeting: A generic message can be a warning sign when a legitimate service normally knows your name.
  • Requests for information: Unexpected requests to verify sensitive information deserve careful scrutiny.
  • Short deadlines: Artificial time pressure is commonly used to discourage verification.

Remember: One clue alone does not prove that an email is malicious. Look at the whole message and verify unexpected requests through an official channel.
