LEARN FROM THE CHALLENGE

What to remember about URLs

🔎

Check the domain

Don't judge a website by familiar words alone. Identify the actual domain you are visiting.

🔒

HTTPS is not a trust badge

HTTPS encrypts communication between your browser and the website, but it does not guarantee that the website is legitimate.

🌐

Understand subdomains

Attackers can use convincing-looking subdomains. Pay attention to where the actual domain ends.

🛡️

Verify independently

If a message asks you to log in or take urgent action, consider navigating to the organization's known website yourself.
