LEARNING NOTES

Why these clues matter

Check the sender

Attackers may use addresses that imitate legitimate organizations. Look carefully at the actual domain.

Slow down when pressured

Urgency and threats are common social-engineering techniques designed to make people act before thinking.

Be careful with account requests

Unexpected requests for passwords, codes, payment details, or account verification deserve extra scrutiny.

Inspect links before clicking

A familiar-looking button does not guarantee that its destination is trustworthy.
